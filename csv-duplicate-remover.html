How to Use

Paste CSV or choose a local .csv file, choose normalization options, select entire-row or column mode, then click Remove Duplicates.

Example

Rows like `Alice,Denver`, ` Alice , Denver`, and `Alice, Denver` are treated as duplicates when trimming is enabled.

Privacy Note

Your CSV is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to a server.

Limitations

  • Very large files may be limited by browser memory.
  • Duplicate detection depends on selected normalization options.
  • In column mode, only the selected column is used to detect duplicates.
