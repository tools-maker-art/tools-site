PDF to CSV Converter

Upload a text-based PDF, extract text from each page, and export lines or simple tables as CSV. This MVP does not include OCR for scanned PDFs.

How It Works

PDF.js reads text items from each page in your browser. The tool groups nearby text items into lines, sorts them left to right, and converts those lines into CSV rows.

Example

Load Example fills sample extracted invoice lines. In Simple Table mode, repeated spaces split each line into CSV columns.

Privacy Note

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your PDF is not uploaded to a server.

Limitations

  • Works best with text-based PDFs.
  • Scanned PDFs need OCR, which is not included.
  • Complex tables may need manual cleanup.
  • Very large PDFs may be limited by browser memory.
