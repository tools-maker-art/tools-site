How It Works

The update CSV is read once into a SKU-to-price map. The main CSV is then scanned once, updating matching prices and leaving unmatched rows unchanged.

Example

Main CSV has `A101,Widget,10.00` and `A102,Part,15.00`. Update CSV has `A101,12.50` and `A999,20.00`. A101 is updated, A102 is unchanged, and A999 is listed as missing.

Privacy Note

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your CSV files are not uploaded.

Limitations

  • Matching depends on SKU consistency.
  • Large files may be limited by browser memory.
